# Google Resume Guide

Keep the resume concise, easy to scan, and focused on accomplishments rather than duties. Put the most relevant experience first and use clear section headings.

Use specific evidence: what was built, the scale or scope, the candidate's contribution, and the measurable result when available. Tailor keywords to the target role while keeping every claim truthful. Remove filler, unexplained acronyms, and outdated details that do not support the role.
