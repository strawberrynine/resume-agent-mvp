# STAR Method

Use the Situation, Task, Action, Result structure for experience bullets.

- Situation: briefly establish the context or problem.
- Task: state the responsibility or objective.
- Action: describe what the candidate personally did, including tools and decisions.
- Result: quantify the outcome when the source resume provides a truthful metric.

Strong bullets lead with an action verb, emphasize individual contribution, and connect the result to business or engineering impact. Never invent metrics or outcomes.
