# AI Engineer Resume Template

Recommended sections:

1. Name and contact links.
2. Short professional summary aligned to the target AI engineering role.
3. Technical skills grouped by languages, ML/LLM, data, cloud, and tooling.
4. Experience with impact-focused STAR bullets.
5. Selected AI projects describing the problem, model or workflow, evaluation, and deployment.
6. Education and relevant certifications.

Make LLM applications concrete: mention retrieval, prompt design, evaluation, latency or cost tradeoffs, orchestration, observability, and deployment only when supported by the original resume.
