# ATS Rules

Use standard headings such as Summary, Skills, Experience, Projects, and Education. Prefer plain Markdown or simple text structure over tables, columns, text boxes, icons, and decorative graphics.

Repeat important job-description terms naturally in the summary, skills, and relevant experience. Use the exact technology name where truthful, include common abbreviations when useful, and avoid keyword stuffing. Keep dates, job titles, and company names consistent and easy to parse.
